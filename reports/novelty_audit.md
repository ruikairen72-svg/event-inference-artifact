# Focused final novelty audit — 2026-10-08

Primary-source search covered event completion, imperfective inference, controlled composition, nonce predicates and explicit process reasoning. This is a focused audit, not an exhaustive novelty guarantee. Sources and local source hashes are in `novelty_sources.json` and the earlier `source_registry.json`.

- Han and Sun's 2026 preprint already includes same-verb VP contrasts and evidence/label diagnostics. Do not claim same-verb pairing as an invention.
- CxMP (Oba and Sugawara, ACL 2026, official full PDF) evaluates nine construction types through minimal pairs, includes human validation and pseudoword substitution, and discusses label/prompt difficulty. Our narrower event study supplies nonce definitions and crosses endpoint evidence. Lexical replacement or minimal-pair evaluation alone is not new.
- CoRE (Vallurupalli and Ferraro, Findings ACL 2025, official full PDF), §5.1/Table 1 and Appendix prompts, already compares ongoing/completed descriptions with and without story context and an unsure option. Context helps some evaluated models and hurts others. Do not claim the first contextual imperfective evaluation.
- COGS, HANS and ProofWriter establish compositional generalization, heuristic diagnostics and rule-defined truth. The finite-history oracle is a narrow application rather than an invention of symbolic benchmarking.

Defensible contribution: joint same-family composition × presentation × explicit endpoint evidence; separation of activity occurrence, culmination and unresolved histories; distinct categorical and subjective-probability measurements; original-gold comparability with published concern flags; and fully provenance-linked contemporary evaluation with paired robustness controls. Presentation gaps cannot by themselves identify internal knowledge. No human-validated or universal linguistic gold claim is made.

# Literature review and claim ledger

Checked 2026-10-07. This is a focused primary-source review, not a systematic review. Downloaded full texts and original source files are hashed in the phase checkpoint. Evidence tiers below distinguish full-text inspection from metadata-only checks. Published results are attributed to their authors; none is a new experiment in this project.

## The two focal works

**Ma & Miyao (2026), ACL long paper 689, pp. 15093–15111.** The [official 19-page PDF](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.689.pdf) and appendices were inspected, alongside the [author version](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.09373v2). The [conference awards page](https://2026.aclweb.org/program/best_papers/) confirms Best Paper. ImperfectiveNLI has 400 items: A interrupted accomplishments, B interrupted activities, C outcome-unspecified accomplishments, D outcome-unspecified activities. Seven 7–9B models: Llama-3.1, Mistral-v0.3, Qwen2.5, DeepSeek-LLM, Gemma-2, GLM-4, Yi-1.5. Prompts: strict zero-shot, definition-aware, CoT, counterfactual. Reported teleological bias is frequent entailment in C; interventions trade this against activity errors. Qwen2.5 scaling spans 1.5/7/14/32/72B; Table 5 is not strictly monotonic. Representation analysis uses BERT cosine similarities, not causal probes of evaluated generators. Three native speakers rated grammar/fluency/adequacy, not independently adjudicated NLI labels. Limits: English/templates, theoretical–human divergence, inference-only interventions, uncontrolled script priors. Paper §8 specifies dataset CC BY-NC 4.0.

**Han & Sun (2026), [arXiv:2608.25005v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.25005v1), submitted August 25.** Full text, tables and appendices inspected; [current abstract record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25005) has no journal reference. Treat as preprint. Aspectual Reduction assigns classes from isolated verbs; other concerns are endpoint rigidity and interruption-to-contradiction mapping. Appendix B lists 76 A items without explicit noncompletion and 97 VSS exclusions, leaving 303. Three background-informed native annotators evaluated all 400 using acceptable labels plus preferred interpretation; 38% A and 29% C permit alternatives by majority support. Their 200 new items already hold verbs constant while crossing VP telicity and outcome evidence. Sufficiency Bias: non-affirmed culmination can coexist with acceptance of simple past. Surface-form Attraction: semantic versus opaque labels change answers under oracle-guided states. Four smaller models plus Qwen2.5-72B/GPT-5.4 are reported; larger-model errors include context-sensitive classification. Limitations include annotation priming, controlled language, simplified theory, and behavioral intermediate outputs.

## Intellectual progression and novelty boundary

The original study motivates a completion-bias diagnostic. The critique questions attribution and already contributes same-verb VP pairs. Our proposed contribution must therefore be the *joint matched design*: VP composition × lexical/knowledge presentation × outcome evidence, a separately controlled plausibility contrast, and prespecified probability versus entailment measurements. Neither same-verb pairs, nonce words, explicit rule worlds, nor prompt interventions alone is new. Do not claim to discover compositional telicity or to locate an internal cognitive module. A final novelty search is required before manuscript writing.

A quantized noun alone does not guarantee VP telicity. |

Incremental theme is the event–object progress mapping, not a synonym for a count object. Quantization and definiteness differ. “Read the book” admits partial-use construals; “push a cart” need not culminate despite a definite object. Boundedness is an operational endpoint condition here, not an exhaustive linguistic theory. No claim that every subinstant satisfies an activity predicate: require a nonzero sufficient witness and exclude onset-only cases.

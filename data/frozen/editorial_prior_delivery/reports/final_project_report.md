## Principal findings

Negative neutral composition scores occur in all six Real systems, but they do not certify categorical inference: unknown endpoints can be entailed or contradicted, and witnessed activity can be rejected. Three systems solve the complete Micro core while other systems fail under the same supplied oracle. In the matched stress comparison, DeepSeek attains perfect Micro accuracy but correctly labels only 14/30 Real unresolved endpoints; its Nonce unknown recall is 27/30. Presentation differences cannot identify causal removal of internal world knowledge.

Eleven of the 30 primary contrasts survive Holm correction. No structural-rule accuracy gain survives that correction. All six main intended-prior point estimates are positive, yet none of the complete 21 prior-context tests survives its secondary BH correction. The three Qwen sizes show nonmonotonic presentation-specific behavior; training/provider differences prevent a scaling-law claim. All unusable mixed-model fits are disclosed, with no inference based on them.

Contemporary original-benchmark accuracy ranges from 53.3–68.0% on unchanged 400-item gold and 67.3–80.2% on the published 303-item subset. These differently composed pools are diagnostics, not paired evidence of improvement. The supplied-concern filter and 24 explicit-noncompletion cases remain separate. The supervised unigram baseline reaches 95.4% core accuracy but loses 16.7 percentage points on the matched held-out renderer, limiting interpretation of controlled-template success.

All inherited passive-form defects in the formal suites received paired corrections: 12/518 labels change, with improvements and regressions both reported; all 20 affected DeepSeek labels remain unchanged. Later service/order differences limit causal attribution. Original outcomes and primary estimates are preserved; no new inferential tests were introduced.
